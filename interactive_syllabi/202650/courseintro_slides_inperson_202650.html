What Is This Course?

A broad exploration of today's digital landscape — designed to make you a more confident, informed, and capable digital citizen.

By the end, you'll be able to…

  • Participate safely and ethically in digital spaces
  • Evaluate information sources critically
  • Design technology-based solutions to real problems
  • Apply modern tools to boost your productivity

What We'll Cover

Information Literacy Tech Product Literacy Intro to Programming Networking Web Literacy Data Literacy Digital Content Creation AI & Machine Learning Cybersecurity & Privacy Digital Citizenship Microsoft 365
